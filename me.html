Three things people ask first

Is Wellet a medical record? A doctor's tool? What is it, exactly?

Wellet is yours — a personal copy of your own health record, pulled from the hospital systems you've already used, organized so you can actually find things. It's not a chart, not a portal, not a doctor's tool. It's a quiet place that belongs to you, where every lab, visit, medication, and note is in one spot, in plain English. You can read it, search it, ask it questions, and hand the relevant parts to any new specialist.

Where does my data live, and who can see it?

Your records live in your encrypted Wellet account, on US-based HIPAA-eligible infrastructure (Supabase + AWS). Only you can read them. We never sell data, never share with advertisers, and never train AI on your records. You can export everything as PDF or delete your account at any time. Full privacy details.

What if my hospital isn't connected yet?

Duke Health is live, MyChart (Epic) hospitals roll out weekly, and Apple Health works out of the box. If your hospital isn't in the list, you can request it from inside the app and we'll email you the moment it's ready — usually a few days. In the meantime you can upload PDFs and CCDA exports from any portal and Wellet will read them too.
