Your data. Your rules.

Wellet connects to your patient portal through SMART on FHIR — the same secure standard hospitals use. Here's exactly what that means.

Read-only access

Wellet reads your health records from MyChart and other patient portals. We never write data back to your EHR. Your provider's records are never modified.

Encrypted and isolated

Every account is encrypted at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS). Row-level security means the database itself enforces that only you can access your data — not even Wellet staff can see it.

You control who sees it

Invite a family member into your record — or don't. Revoke access instantly. Export or delete everything at any time. There is no lock-in.

No selling. No ads. Ever.

We don't sell your data, use third-party pixels, or work with data brokers. AI features process your data in real time and return insights — nothing is stored for training.

Frequently asked questions

Is Wellet a medical record? A doctor's tool? What is it, exactly?

Wellet is yours — a personal copy of your own health record, pulled from the hospital systems you've already used, organized so you can actually find things. It's not a chart, not a portal, not a doctor's tool. It's a quiet place that belongs to you, where every lab, visit, medication, and note is in one spot, in plain English. You can read it, search it, ask it questions, and hand the relevant parts to any new specialist.

How is Wellet different from MyChart?

Wellet works with MyChart, not as a replacement for it. MyChart is built for the hospital. Wellet is built for you — the person reading the chart at 11 PM trying to figure out if something changed. We pull your records from MyChart and other portals through SMART on FHIR (the same secure standard hospitals use) and translate them into what you actually need to know: what drifted, what's new, what the next visit should cover.

Where does my data live, and who can see it?

Your records live in your encrypted Wellet account, on US-based HIPAA-eligible infrastructure (Supabase + AWS). Only you can read them. We never sell data, never share with advertisers, and never train AI on your records. You can export everything as PDF or delete your account at any time. Full privacy details.

Does Wellet work with Apple Health?

Yes. Wellet reads from Apple Health on iPhone — resting heart rate, steps, sleep, and any wearable you've already connected (Apple Watch, Oura, Fitbit). Your wearable rhythm sits next to your hospital records in one quiet view so you can see them in context, not in seven different apps.

What if my hospital isn't connected yet?

Duke Health is live, MyChart (Epic) hospitals roll out weekly, and Apple Health works out of the box. If your hospital isn't in the list, you can request it from inside the app and we'll email you the moment it's ready — usually a few days. In the meantime you can upload PDFs and CCDA exports from any portal and Wellet will read them too.

Can I export everything?

Yes — any time, for any reason. Export your full record as PDF or CCDA from inside the app. No lock-in, no support ticket, no waiting. Take it to a new specialist, hand it to a family member, or just keep a copy for yourself.

What does it cost, and how do I cancel?

Free, forever. No tiers, no paywall, no credit card. Wellet is built to be a gift to people managing their own care — and the caregivers who often are them too. We sustain it through foundation grants, mission-aligned partner licenses, and a separate product called Wellet Pro for self-trackers who want personal genomics, pedigree rendering, or hereditary scans (pay-per-use credit packs at /pricing).

Does Wellet replace my doctor?

No. Wellet is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. It's a quiet record of your own data, with plain-language summaries and pattern notices to help you have better conversations with your care team. Always consult your healthcare provider for medical questions.
