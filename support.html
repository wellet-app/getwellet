Quick answers to the questions we hear most. Can't find what you need? Email hello@getwellet.com and we'll get back to you within a business day.

Getting started

My hospital isn't showing up when I try to connect

Wellet can connect to most U.S. health systems that use Epic MyChart, plus the VA. We're adding hospitals as quickly as their FHIR endpoints come online.

If you search for your hospital and don't see it:

  • Try the parent health system name (e.g., "Duke Health" instead of "Duke Heart Center").
  • Check that the hospital has a MyChart patient portal — if they don't, Wellet can't connect to it yet.
  • Email hello@getwellet.com with your hospital name and we'll prioritize adding it.

You can still use Wellet without a hospital connection — upload documents (discharge summaries, After Visit Summaries, lab PDFs) and Wellet reads and organizes them.

I never got my sign-in email (magic link)

Magic-link emails usually arrive within 30 seconds. If yours hasn't:

  • Check your spam, promotions, or junk folder — first-time emails sometimes land there.
  • Search your inbox for noreply@getwellet.com.
  • Confirm you typed the email correctly. Each link is bound to one email address.
  • Magic links expire after 1 hour. If yours has, request a new one from the sign-in screen.

Still nothing? Email hello@getwellet.com from the address you tried to sign in with and we'll look it up.

How do I share Wellet with a family member?

Open Wellet, tap the loved one's name at the top, then tap Care Circle › Invite. Enter your family member's email and they'll get an invitation to view the same records.

Care circle members see what you see — records, CareSignals, and updates. Only you can connect or disconnect hospitals.

How do I delete my account?

Open Wellet, go to Settings › Account › Delete my account. Confirm, and we permanently delete:

  • Your profile and authentication.
  • All medical records, documents, and notes for every loved one on your account.
  • Care circle invitations you've sent.
  • Any backups within 30 days (encrypted, no human access).

This can't be undone. If you only want to disconnect a hospital, you can do that without deleting your account: Records › Manage connections.

If you can't access the app, email privacy@getwellet.com from the address on your account and we'll delete it for you.

What Wellet does (and doesn't do)

Is Wellet HIPAA-compliant?

Wellet handles protected health information (PHI) for our users and follows HIPAA-aligned security practices: encryption in transit and at rest, audit logging, role-based access, and Business Associate Agreements with subprocessors that touch PHI.

The honest framing: HIPAA primarily governs covered entities (hospitals, insurers, clinical providers). When you use Wellet for your own loved one, you control the data. We treat it with hospital-grade security regardless.

For details on what we collect, where it's stored, and how it's protected, see our Privacy Policy.

Does Wellet give medical advice?

No. Wellet reads and organizes medical records and surfaces patterns you might want to discuss with a clinician — it does not diagnose, treat, or replace medical advice.

CareSignals notice things like "blood pressure trending up over the last 3 visits" or "this medication was added without a documented stop date." They are observations from public clinical data, not recommendations.

Always consult a qualified healthcare provider with questions about a medical condition or before making any care decision.

What is Wellet Connect?

Wellet Connect is the secure bridge that connects your loved one's hospital portal (like Epic MyChart) to Wellet. You sign in with the portal credentials you already have — Wellet never sees your password. The hospital returns a permission token that lets Wellet read records on your loved one's behalf.

You can revoke that permission any time from Records › Manage connections, or directly from your hospital portal.

Connecting through Wellet Connect requires that you already have proxy access to your loved one's portal — that's an arrangement you set up with the hospital, not us.

How much does Wellet cost?

Wellet is free during our early access period. We will introduce paid plans later this year for premium features (longer history, more care circle members, advanced exports). Anyone who signs up during early access keeps free access to the current feature set.

We will never sell your health data — full stop. Pricing details: getwellet.com/#pricing.

Feedback & bugs

How do I report a bug or request a feature?

The fastest way: open Wellet, tap Settings › Send feedback. We see every report — usually within an hour during business hours.

You can also email hello@getwellet.com. Screenshots and the steps you took before the issue happened make a huge difference.

Where is Wellet built?

Wellet is built in North Carolina by a small founding team. Our cloud infrastructure (Supabase, Netlify) is U.S.-hosted. Read more about how we operate on the transparency page.

Still stuck? Email hello@getwellet.com with as much detail as you can share. We read every message.
