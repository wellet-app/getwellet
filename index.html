How it works

Three things, done well.

See it in one place

Connect the hospital portal or add records yourself. Visits, labs and medicines land on one timeline, in the order they happened.

Walk in ready

Before an appointment, Wellet shows what changed since last time and the questions worth asking. Ask Wellet answers from the record, with sources.

Keep it in the family

Invite the people who help. Wishes, the care circle and an emergency summary stay with the family, and you choose who sees what.

Your record, your rules

Wellet reads the record. It never writes to it.

  • Read-onlyWellet connects through SMART on FHIR, the standard hospitals use, and never changes the chart.
  • No ads, no data salesWe don't sell, share or advertise against your family's health data.
  • Yours to keepExport or delete everything at any time. Records already in Wellet are never removed because a plan ends.
