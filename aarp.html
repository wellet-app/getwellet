Witness, don't monitor.

A three-minute look at how Wellet helps family caregivers see everything that matters about their loved one's health, in one place.

Length3 min 10 sec
CaptionsOpen · burned in
FounderBetsy Eble

I'm Betsy. Twenty-three years ago I designed Duke Health's first electronic medical record. When it was my own mother, there was still no software for me. So I built Wellet.

Wellet pulls medical records across every hospital a loved one has been seen at — Epic, VA Lighthouse, Oracle Cerner, athenahealth, MEDITECH — alongside wearables and Apple Health. It gives the caregiver one calm view: what changed, what's next, what to ask.

Built April through June 2026, in production, connected to 482 health systems.
