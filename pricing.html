Common questions

What's free?
Adding loved ones, visits, medicines and wishes. The care circle and the emergency summary. One complete import from each loved one's hospital, so you see their whole history before you pay anything. Each loved one's first summary. Five questions and three document reads a month. Nothing you add is ever taken away.
What does the $10 for automatic updates do?
After the first import, new hospital records keep coming in on their own, so you don't have to upload anything yourself. You pay once for each loved one. There's no renewal.
Do I need Wellet AI?
No. Free covers the record itself. Wellet AI is for caregivers who ask questions often, want updated summaries, or read a lot of documents. One subscription covers everyone you care for, and you can cancel anytime.
Why charge once for updates but monthly for AI?
Keeping a record updated costs us very little, so a single payment covers it. The AI costs us money every time it reads a record or answers a question, so it's a monthly plan.
I signed up early. What happens to me?
Anyone who signed up before this page went live gets automatic updates free for their first loved one. That was the promise, and it stands. Founding Members keep everything they were promised. If you're not sure what applies to you, email hello@mywellet.com.
When will I be asked to pay?
Not while we launch. When the paid options arrive, you'll only see them after the first import, and "Not now" is always a full-size choice. On iPhone, you'll see Apple's standard purchase sheet.
What happens to my records if I get a refund or stop paying?
They stay. Records already in Wellet are never removed because of a refund, a cancellation or a disconnected hospital. Only the automatic updates or the AI features stop.
What is Wellet Pro?
Wellet Pro is a separate product for people doing deep personal work with their own health data — genomic imports, pedigree rendering, hereditary pattern scans. It's sold in pay-per-use credit packs because those features cost real compute to run. If you're a caregiver, you do not need Pro. If you're a self-tracker who wants those tools, Pro is built for you.
What does a Wellet Pro credit actually buy?
A credit is Wellet Pro's unit of compute. Heavier operations — a full genomic import, a hereditary pattern scan across a curated variant set, a multi-generation pedigree render — spend more credits than light ones like previews and single-variant lookups. Every operation shows its exact credit cost before it runs, so you never spend without seeing the number first. Credits never expire. Starter (100 credits) is meant for trying one full workflow, Standard (300) for ongoing work, Power (750) for serial family-history and rare-disease work.
Can I try Wellet before signing up?
Yes. You can try the live document parser at mywellet.com/try without signing up. Drop a discharge summary, see what Wellet reads in seconds.
Does Wellet sell my data?
Never. We don't sell, share, or advertise against your health data. The only people who see your loved one's records are the people you invite into the care circle. Read the full transparency report.
How does Wellet stay sustainable?
Three sources: the $10 automatic updates for each loved one, the optional Wellet AI subscription that covers what the AI costs us to run, and the separate Wellet Pro credit product. Foundation grants and mission-aligned partner licenses backstop the mission side of the work. Read the build journal.
What if $10 is a hardship?
Email hello@mywellet.com and we will cover automatic updates and Wellet AI at no cost. No questions, no proof required. Caregiving is hard enough.
Can I get a refund?
Yes. On iPhone, Apple handles refunds. Email hello@mywellet.com and we'll help. A refund never removes records already in Wellet.
Who is Wellet for?
Adult children caregivers, spouses caring for a loved one, anyone navigating a parent's or family member's healthcare while holding down their own life. If you've ever found yourself logging into a portal at 11pm trying to remember what the doctor said three weeks ago — Wellet is built for you.
