Common questions

Can I try Wellet before I pay?
Yes — the Free plan never expires, and you can also try the live document parser at mywellet.com/try without signing up. Drop a discharge summary, see what Wellet reads in seconds.
What's the difference between Plus and Pro?
Plus covers everything most caregivers need: AI summaries, pattern alerts, document extraction, and Ask Wellet personalized to your loved one. Pro adds live EHR integration (Wellet pulls records straight from MyChart/Epic), wearable data, and multiple care recipients — useful if you're caring for more than one person or want continuous syncing instead of manual uploads.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. Monthly plans cancel at the next billing date. Yearly plans are prorated if you cancel within the first 30 days; after that, your subscription runs to the end of the year you've paid for. No questions asked.
Does Wellet sell my data?
Never. Wellet runs on a HIPAA-grade architecture and we don't sell, share, or advertise against your health data. The only people who see your loved one's records are the people you invite into the care circle. Read the full transparency report.
Why is Wellet so much cheaper than I'd expect?
Wellet was built by one caregiver with AI tools, not a 30-person engineering team. That's the whole point of the Billion Dollar Build — proving that one person who's lived the problem can ship a real product. Lower overhead means lower prices. Read the build journal.
Who is Wellet for?
Adult children caregivers, spouses caring for a loved one, anyone navigating a parent's or family member's healthcare while holding down their own life. If you've ever found yourself logging into a portal at 11pm trying to remember what the doctor said three weeks ago — Wellet is built for you.
